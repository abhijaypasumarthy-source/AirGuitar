The Development Journey

Method 1: Push Button

Status: Rejected

Unnatural feel. Did not accurately simulate guitar playing biomechanics.

Method 2: Accelerometer (AX, AY, AZ)

Status: Rejected

Generated false positives. Normal arm movements and gravity triggered false strums.

Method 3: Gyroscope (GY)

Status: Selected

Strumming is a wrist rotation. Tracking angular velocity isolated the strum perfectly.

Future Scope

  • Up-Strum & Down-Strum Detection
  • Velocity Sensitive Playing
  • AI-Based Gesture Recognition
